    What this is

    A versioned dataset of kṛtis by Saint Tyāgarāja (1767–1847) — rāga, tāla, language, pallavi and a short gloss for each — wrapped in a study app. Rāga scales are cross-checked against melakarta arithmetic in CI, so a wrong ārōhaṇa cannot land in the data. Everything you mark, note or practise stays in this browser's localStorage; nothing is uploaded.

    Read the fine print

    Rāga and tāla attributions follow commonly cited editions and differ between paṭhāntarams — treat a row as a starting point for your guru's version, not as the last word. Coverage is a working subset of the ~700 attributed kṛtis; see README.md for provenance and how to add entries.
